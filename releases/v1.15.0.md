Round 12 runs Dexy-Decision-9B, an independent open-weight fine-tune of Qwen3.5-9B requested by its developer, on all 3,250 text cases. It ran on a rented A100 GPU through the maker's own vLLM adapter, with the same request bodies as every other model. It also adds in-domain tags: models that trained on another split of a dataset we test are now marked, with their index without those datasets.

## New on the board
| Model | Maker | System One Index | Without in-domain datasets | Calibration error | Median latency, GPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dexy-Decision-9B | Patrick-18 | 84.7 (1st of 38) | 83.4 (7th, 12 datasets) | 3.5% | 62 ms |

- **The top index, but not clearly the best model.** It leads Decider V1.2 by 0.07 points, and 4 of its 16 datasets are in-domain: it was fine-tuned on the train splits of typed-decisions and Banking77, and on MASSIVE in English and Chinese, the labels of our Arabic MASSIVE datasets. On the other 12 it averages 83.4, 7th.
- **None of our test cases are in its training splits**, and our run matches the developer's own (84.82 reported, 84.71 measured). Every call succeeded.
- **Text only**, so no vision score. Its latency is local (a GPU, one call at a time, no network), so it stays out of the latency charts like the other open-weight models.

## In-domain tags
A model whose maker says it trained on another split of a dataset we test now has an **in-domain** tag on the leaderboard. Its row and page list those datasets and give its index without them, ranked against every model on the same datasets. The System One Index still counts all 16 datasets for every model. Tagged: Dexy-Decision-9B, Kev 4B and 0.8B, Naluri-308M and 279M, laya-ara and laya-ara-rag. Models that don't publish their training data may overlap too, and carry no tag.

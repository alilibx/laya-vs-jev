Round 12 runs Dexy-Decision-9B, an independent open-weight fine-tune of Qwen3.5-9B requested by its developer, on all 3,250 text cases. It ran on a rented A100 GPU through the maker's own vLLM adapter, with the same request bodies as every other model. It goes straight to 1st on the System One Index.

## New on the board
| Model | Maker | System One Index | Rank | Calibration error | Median latency, GPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dexy-Decision-9B | Patrick-18 | **84.7** | 1st of 38 | 3.5% | 62 ms |

- **1st by 0.1 points**, ahead of Decider V1.2 (84.6), and 2nd on the Arabic index. Every call succeeded.
- **Two datasets are in-domain.** It was fine-tuned on the train splits of typed-decisions and Banking77, and on MASSIVE's English and Chinese train splits. Without typed-decisions and Banking77 it averages 84.5, 0.6 behind Decider V1.2.
- **It matches the developer's own run**: 84.82 reported, 84.71 measured here.
- **Text only**, so no vision score. Its latency is local (a GPU, one call at a time, no network), so it stays out of the latency charts like the other open-weight models.
